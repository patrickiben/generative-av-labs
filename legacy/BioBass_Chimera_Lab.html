Play the drone, then drag the sub phase: near 180° the sub and the patch's low fundamental fall out of phase and the <120 Hz energy drops sharply into a deep notch; auto-align sweeps every angle and locks the one that hits hardest. The ✓/✗ badge is read from the measured RMS, not the knob angle.
This is the genuinely clever part of the blueprint, made real: the validator measures <120 Hz RMS of (lead-low + sub) as it rotates the sub's phase, then locks the maximum — constructive interference. In the full rig this same logic runs as a Max pfft~/allpass~ device so your drop never thins out on a club system.

⑦ What's real vs. what the full pipeline adds read me

✅ Actually running in this page

  • Real-time synthesis — FM core, formant banks, noise transients, ADSR, the wub LFO.
  • Morph = displacement — formant frequencies log-interpolate (mass moves along frequency), the OT idea, not an amplitude crossfade.
  • A correct phase cross-validator — it measures <120 Hz RMS vs sub phase and locks the constructive maximum. You can hear it work.
  • The full power stack — saturation (WaveShaper), OTT-style (downward) glue compression, fizz, and an offline WAV render.
  • A reactive synesthetic visualizer driven by the live FFT + morph state.
  • Polyphony — up to 5 detuned unison voices + chord stacks, each its own FM/formant Brain; the sub & phase-validator stay single and anchored to the root, so the boom-null is still real under a chord.
  • Audio-file import — decode any WAV/mp3 offline (decodeAudioData): estimate its formants into an 8th "sample" animal you can morph, or loop it as the oscillator through the formant/power chain. Estimated formants are a heuristic fit, not a voice clone.

🧪 What the HuggingFace / M4L + TD build adds

  • Diffusion (AudioLDM / Stable Audio) — hallucinates new texture between the two sounds instead of interpolating parameters.
  • Sinkhorn OT on real spectrograms — true Wasserstein transport between two recorded .wavs, not preset formants.
  • Gemma 4 — actual language→species curation and per-frame "director" decisions.
  • Max for Live + TouchDesigner — OSC routing, the GLSL Wasserstein-warp sculpture (lobes scaled by the W₂ displacement distance), and hands-on XY-pad performance.
  • Fidelity gap: log-interp morphs the formants you specified but can't invent the breathy, grainy in-between timbre a duck-wolf would actually have — AudioLDM / Stable Audio hallucinate that new texture; you'll hear the difference as "clean synth" vs "organic."

Everything labeled live is honest DSP you're hearing. Everything labeled OT is a faithful parametric stand-in for the heavy model. Nothing here pretends to be a diffusion model or an LLM — it's the playable proof-of-concept that the whole Chimera Engine is buildable.

▸ Reference — the real Sinkhorn-OT step this page stands in for 
# Entropic OT between two magnitude spectra a, b (length-n, sum=1).
# Cost on the LOG-frequency axis: C[i][j] = (log f_i - log f_j)^2
K = exp(-C/eps)                       # Gibbs kernel
u = ones(n)
for it in 1..L:                       # Sinkhorn iterations
    v = b / (K.T @ u)
    u = a / (K   @ v)
P = diag(u) @ K @ diag(v)             # transport plan
T[i] = sum_j P[i][j]*f_j / sum_j P[i][j]   # barycentric map = where bin i slides

For 1-D histograms the exact (eps→0) optimal map is just the inverse-CDF coupling — quantile matching — which is why this page's log-frequency formant interpolation is a legitimate cheap stand-in for full Sinkhorn on 2-D spectrograms. Caveat: the page also interpolates formant amplitude/Q, so mass isn't strictly preserved — the closed form is exact for the frequency-axis displacement, approximate for the full magnitude spectrum.
