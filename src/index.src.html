Instruments for light and sound, in the browser

Play it, walk into it, breed it, forge it, blend it.

Each page runs in your browser, with no helper app and no server.

Nebula

The last twelve seconds of your playing hang in the air. Every hit is born at the core and winds outward, and each drum stirs the field its own way.

Open Nebula →

Biofeedback Nebula

Nebula driven by your heart rate or your drums. An inferred score warms the gas, and paced breathing gathers the particles into a ring. A heart-rate sensor connects over Bluetooth in Chrome or Edge; the Muse S modes run here only as a labeled synthetic preview.

Open Biofeedback Nebula →

Enter

Walk into a picture and fly through a sound, then cast the sound into a printable sculpture. Depth in your own picture is a learned estimate, labeled as one.

Open Enter →

Chimera Synth

A bass you breed by ear. Drag a bud to evolve the sound, morph between fifteen animal voices, and play it from a MIDI keyboard or your computer keys. Real synthesis in this browser, with no model.

Open Chimera Synth →

Detail Forge Analog

Keep the recording as an anchor and forge a new layer of fine texture. Set the protected band, shape the synthesis, and hear just the detail you added. Source-adaptive, entirely in your browser.

Open Detail Forge Analog →

Displacer

Don't crossfade, move the mass. Two sounds blend by optimal transport, so a resonance slides along frequency instead of fading, with a labeled spectrogram and listening that follows the slider. Explore their atoms in 3D, or control the blend with a mod wheel. No model.

Open Displacer →
